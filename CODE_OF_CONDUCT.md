# Code of Conduct

Contributors must communicate respectfully, welcome different backgrounds and
experience levels, and keep reviews focused on observable behavior and evidence.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and publishing
private information without permission are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to a maintainer through GitHub. If no private channel
is available, request one without disclosing sensitive details in a public issue.
Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect privacy, and may remove abusive
content or restrict participation. See MAINTAINERS.md for the responsible people.
